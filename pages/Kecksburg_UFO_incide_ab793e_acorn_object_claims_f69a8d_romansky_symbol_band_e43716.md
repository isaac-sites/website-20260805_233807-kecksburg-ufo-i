## Introduction

The famous claim that the Kecksburg object bore “Egyptian hieroglyphics” is more limited than many later retellings suggest. The description traces primarily to one witness, volunteer fireman James Romansky, who said he saw unfamiliar markings on a raised band or “bumper” near one end of the acorn-shaped object. He did not claim that the entire object was covered in writing, nor did he identify the symbols as genuine Egyptian characters. Instead, he repeatedly explained that the markings merely *looked to him* like ancient hieroglyphics because they were unlike any lettering or identification he recognised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved Mysteries...</span></span></span>

That distinction matters because the alleged symbols have become one of the defining features of the Kecksburg story, despite the absence of any authenticated photograph, sketch made at the time, rubbing, or surviving [physical evidence]({{ 'physical-evidence/' | relative_url }}). Modern depictions of elaborate alien writing largely reflect artistic interpretation rather than documented observation.

## Romansky's published description

The best-known account comes from Romansky's interviews for the 1990 television programme *[Unsolved Mysteries]({{ 'tv-prop/' | relative_url }})*. Describing the object, he said there were no wings, engines, propellers or conventional aircraft markings. His attention was drawn instead to a protruding section on the lower rear portion, which he called the "bumper".

According to Romansky:

> "On that bumper there was what I call, it looked to me like the ancient Egyptian hieroglyphics. It was markings like stars, and shapes and figures and circles, and lines."

His wording is careful. Rather than asserting that the markings were Egyptian writing, he presented "hieroglyphics" as a visual comparison. He immediately qualified the statement by listing simple geometric forms—stars, circles, lines and other shapes—and concluded that he had never seen anything similar before.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved Mysteries...</span></span></span>

Later interviews collected by investigator [Stan Gordon]({{ 'stan-gordon/' | relative_url }}) remain broadly consistent with this description. Gordon reported that Romansky described the symbols as being confined to the raised band near one end of an otherwise smooth, bronze-gold object without visible rivets or seams. Gordon also noted Romansky's background as an experienced machinist, a detail often included to explain why Romansky believed the object's construction appeared unusual.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/kecksburg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on stangordon.info.</span></span></span>

## Why the "symbol band" became more famous than the original account

As the Kecksburg incident entered UFO literature, Romansky's restrained description gradually expanded in popular retellings.

Several changes commonly appeared:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* The comparison with hieroglyphics became simplified into claims that the object **had Egyptian hieroglyphics**.
* Marks confined to a raised band became descriptions of symbols covering much of the craft.
* Simple geometric forms—stars, circles and lines—were increasingly illustrated as elaborate alien writing.
* Later artwork often presented continuous rows of complex glyphs even though no witness produced such a drawing from 1965.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/kecksburg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on stangordon.info.</span></span></span>

</div>

These embellishments are understandable from a storytelling perspective. "Unknown writing" is visually memorable, and artists producing reconstructions needed to invent designs because no verified image existed. Over time, those artistic reconstructions have often been mistaken for documentary evidence.

## What the evidence actually supports

Taken literally, Romansky's testimony supports only a limited number of conclusions.

It supports that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* he believed he saw unfamiliar markings;
* the markings appeared on a raised band or bumper near one end of the object;
* he described them using comparisons with stars, circles, lines and figures;
* he considered them unlike any aircraft identification he recognised.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved Mysteries...</span></span></span>

</div>

It does **not** independently establish:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* that the markings formed a writing system;
* that they resembled authentic Egyptian hieroglyphs in any linguistic sense;
* that they encircled the entire object;
* that multiple independent witnesses gave equally detailed descriptions of the same symbols.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thecoldfile.com/articles/1965-kecksburg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thecoldfile.com">[The Cold File]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thecoldfile.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1965 kecksburg</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Cold FileThe Acorn in the Woods: The 1965 Kecksburg Incident &#124; The Cold File...</span></span></span>

This distinction is frequently lost in summaries that simply refer to "hieroglyphics on the Kecksburg UFO."

## What cannot be tested without images

The largest obstacle to evaluating the claim is the complete absence of contemporary visual documentation.

No authenticated photograph of the alleged object has been released. Likewise, there is no verified field sketch by Romansky, no tracing of the markings, no measurements of their size or spacing, and no preserved fragment showing the reported band. As a result, researchers cannot examine questions such as:

* whether the marks were engraved, embossed or painted;
* whether they formed a repeating decorative pattern or unique symbols;
* whether they could have been manufacturing marks from an aerospace component;
* whether different witnesses would have described the same shapes independently.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thecoldfile.com/articles/1965-kecksburg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thecoldfile.com">[The Cold File]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thecoldfile.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1965 kecksburg</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Cold FileThe Acorn in the Woods: The 1965 Kecksburg Incident &#124; The Cold File...</span></span></span>

Without visual evidence, every reconstruction of the symbol band is necessarily interpretative rather than documentary.

## Why historians treat the claim cautiously

The symbol band remains one of the most discussed features of the Kecksburg narrative because it is distinctive and memorable. Yet it also illustrates the difference between eyewitness testimony and verifiable evidence.

Romansky appears to have remained consistent in describing unfamiliar marks on a localised band of the object over many years, and there is no indication that he later transformed his own account into claims of extensive alien writing. The broader mythology surrounding Kecksburg instead grew through repeated retellings, artistic reconstructions and secondary accounts that gradually elevated a cautious visual comparison into a claim of mysterious hieroglyphic inscriptions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/2021/08/21/kecksburg-ufo-witness-james-romansky-passes-away/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[stangordon.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on stangordon.info.</span></span></span>

For that reason, the strongest evidence available is narrower than the popular legend. The historical record supports one witness's report of unusual geometric markings on a raised band near one end of the alleged object. It does not provide the photographs or contemporaneous drawings that would allow those markings to be independently analysed or verified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[unsolved.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved Mysteries...</span></span></span>

    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
