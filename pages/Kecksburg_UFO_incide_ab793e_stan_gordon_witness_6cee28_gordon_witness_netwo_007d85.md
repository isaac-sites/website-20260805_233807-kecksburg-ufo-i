## Introduction

Stan Gordon's importance to the [Kecksburg UFO incident]({{ 'kecksburg-ufo-incident/' | relative_url }}) lies less in discovering new [physical evidence]({{ 'physical-evidence/' | relative_url }}) than in building an unusually extensive civilian witness network across western Pennsylvania. Beginning with his interest as a teenager after the events of 9 December 1965, Gordon spent decades identifying, interviewing and re-interviewing people who believed they had seen some part of the incident. Rather than relying on a handful of famous eyewitnesses, he attempted to reconstruct the event by assembling many independent perspectives from local residents, volunteer firefighters, police officers, journalists, military personnel and later informants. This long-term witness-hunting effort became the foundation on which most modern civilian accounts of the Kecksburg case rest.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/kecksburg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneKecksburg Incident and Updates – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneJanuary 18, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2012</span></span></span>

The network is significant because it [preserved testimony]({{ 'preserved-testimony/' | relative_url }}) that might otherwise have disappeared as witnesses aged or died. At the same time, it raises important historical questions. Most interviews occurred years after the incident, and many of the strongest claims depend on recollections collected decades later rather than contemporaneous records. Understanding Gordon's network therefore means examining both how he found witnesses and how historians should evaluate the resulting body of testimony.

## From teenage listener to field investigator

Gordon has consistently described hearing radio reports of the Kecksburg incident while he was still a teenager in nearby Greensburg. Although he was not present at the recovery site, the event became one of the central investigations of his career. Over subsequent decades he developed contacts throughout Westmoreland County and neighbouring communities, eventually becoming one of Pennsylvania's best-known civilian investigators of UFO reports and other unexplained phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/kecksburg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneKecksburg Incident and Updates – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneJanuary 18, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2012</span></span></span>

Unlike investigators who concentrated on archival documents, Gordon relied heavily on local relationships. He repeatedly returned to Kecksburg, attended community events, spoke with residents who had previously avoided publicity and followed leads generated by earlier interviews. Because western Pennsylvania's volunteer fire companies, local newspapers and small communities maintained long personal connections, one witness often introduced him to another. The resulting network expanded gradually rather than through a single organised investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/news-events/page/2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on stangordon.info.</span></span></span>

This approach reflected an oral-history model. Gordon viewed every participant—whether a direct observer or someone who witnessed only part of the military response—as potentially contributing another piece of a larger chronology.

## Residents, firefighters and military informants

One distinctive feature of Gordon's investigation is the breadth of people he attempted to locate rather than the prominence of any single witness.

His interviews broadly fell into several overlapping groups:

* **Local residents**, who reported seeing the fireball, observing its descent or approaching the wooded area before military personnel secured it.
* **Volunteer firefighters**, who responded believing an aircraft had crashed and later described search operations, military roadblocks and, in some cases, a metallic object.
* **Motorists and bystanders**, whose accounts focused on traffic, military vehicles or access restrictions.
* **Journalists and radio personnel**, especially those connected with the original WHJB broadcasts that first spread news of the incident.
* **Former military personnel and civilian informants**, who claimed knowledge of transportation, handling or subsequent movement of the recovered object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/kecksburg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneKecksburg Incident and Updates – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneJanuary 18, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 18, 2012</span></span></span>

The best-known witnesses illustrate how Gordon expanded the network over time.

Bill Bulebush became one of the principal civilian observers in Gordon's reconstruction. According to Gordon's interviews, Bulebush reported seeing the descending object, following it towards the wooded area and briefly observing an unusual acorn-shaped object before military personnel secured the location.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/2015/12/04/kecksburg-ufo-witness-says-object-was-not-ge-mark-2-reentry-vehicle-2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneKecksburg UFO Witness Says Object Was Not GE Mark 2 Reentry Vehicle – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneDec...</span></span></span>

James Romansky represents another important category within the network. Although not a Kecksburg resident, he responded as a volunteer firefighter and later described approaching what he believed was a large seamless metallic object partially embedded in the ground. Gordon first met Romansky in 1987, more than twenty years after the incident, and his testimony became central to later public presentations and television documentaries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/2021/08/21/kecksburg-ufo-witness-james-romansky-passes-away/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneKecksburg UFO Witness- James Romansky- Passes Away – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneAugust 21, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2021</span></span></span>

## How Gordon expanded the network over time

The witness network was not assembled immediately after 1965. Instead, it developed across several decades.

Several factors contributed to its growth:

* Many witnesses reportedly feared ridicule and chose not to speak publicly until the 1980s or 1990s.
* Increased media attention, especially television documentaries and *[Unsolved Mysteries]({{ 'tv-prop/' | relative_url }})*, encouraged additional people to come forward.
* Earlier interviews generated referrals, allowing Gordon to locate relatives, neighbours and former emergency responders who had never previously been interviewed.
* Gordon sometimes withheld details from publication until he believed separate witnesses had independently confirmed them, arguing that this reduced the risk of contaminating testimony.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/news-events/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneSighting Reports &amp; NEWS – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneJuly 8, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 8, 2026</span></span></span>

## What "hundreds of witnesses" actually includes

One of Gordon's most frequently quoted statements is that he interviewed "hundreds of witnesses" or people with information about Kecksburg. The phrase is easily misunderstood.

Gordon's own descriptions indicate that this figure was never limited to people claiming direct observation of the alleged object. Instead, the broader network included individuals with very different relationships to the incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/news-events/page/2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on stangordon.info.</span></span></span>

These categories include:

* direct eyewitnesses to the fireball or landing;
* people who reached the wooded search area;
* volunteer firefighters and emergency personnel;
* police officers and journalists;
* residents who observed military activity;
* individuals reporting conversations with military personnel;
* later informants claiming knowledge of transport or storage;
* secondary witnesses who supplied contextual information rather than first-hand observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/news-events/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneSighting Reports &amp; NEWS – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneJuly 8, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 8, 2026</span></span></span>

## Strengths and limitations of the witness network

From a historical perspective, Gordon's network has several notable strengths.

His long-term engagement meant that many ageing witnesses were interviewed before their deaths, preserving memories that otherwise would likely have been lost. The network also contains accounts from people occupying different positions around the incident, allowing researchers to compare overlapping descriptions instead of relying on a single narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/buy-stuff-here/kecksburg-the-untold-story/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneKecksburg: The Untold Story – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneJanuary 20, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 20, 2012</span></span></span>

However, the same network also presents methodological challenges.

Most testimony was collected years or decades after 1965, increasing the possibility of normal memory change, unintentional influence from media coverage or conversations with others, and retrospective interpretation. Likewise, many of Gordon's interview notes remain part of his personal archive rather than a formally catalogued public collection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stangordon.info/wp/2021/08/21/kecksburg-ufo-witness-james-romansky-passes-away/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stangordon.info">[Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies Zone]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stangordon.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneKecksburg UFO Witness- James Romansky- Passes Away – Stan Gordon&#x27;s UFO Anomalies ZoneAugust 21, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 21, 2021</span></span></span>
